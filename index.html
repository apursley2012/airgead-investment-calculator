Compound interest calculator

Master the art of compound growth.

Airgead is a user-friendly, static compound interest calculator app designed to help individuals compare investment growth scenarios with and without monthly deposits. By illustrating how compound interest accumulates over time, the app enables users to understand the significant impact of consistent contributions on their savings and investment growth, promoting better financial planning.
