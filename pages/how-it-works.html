Compound interest logic

How Airgead calculates growth.

Airgead uses a monthly compounding model to compare a starting balance alone against a starting balance with recurring monthly deposits.

The calculation

Each month, the current balance is multiplied by the monthly interest rate. For the deposit scenario, the monthly deposit is then added after interest is applied for that month.

Monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 100 ÷ 12.
That monthly rate is applied twelve times for each year in the projection.

Why the comparison matters

The calculator keeps two balances running at the same time: one with monthly deposits and one without. That makes the extra value from consistent contributions visible instead of buried inside one final number.

Static demo behavior

This version does not need a backend. Current calculations and saved plans are stored in localStorage, and CSV exports are generated directly in the browser.
