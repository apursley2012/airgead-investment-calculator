Illustrative calculations, not predictions

Airgead estimates growth using a starting balance, monthly deposits, an assumed annual rate, and a chosen timeframe. It applies the same monthly rate throughout the projection and adds each deposit before calculating interest.

Actual returns are not fixed. Investment values may rise or fall, and a projected balance should not be treated as a forecast or guaranteed outcome.

Factors not included in the model

The calculation does not model fees, taxes, inflation, changing market conditions, withdrawals, variable contributions, or changes in interest rates. It also does not assess whether a particular investment is suitable for you.

Amounts are displayed in currency format for convenience. They are not official account statements, tax calculations, or contractual interest figures.

Making financial decisions

Consider your financial obligations, cash reserves, debt, investment risk, and time horizon before making decisions. Seek qualified financial or tax advice when your circumstances require it.

Airgead is an educational software project. It does not provide individual investment advice or recommend financial products.
