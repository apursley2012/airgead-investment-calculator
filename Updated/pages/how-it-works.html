Enter your investment assumptions

Enter an initial balance, a monthly contribution, an assumed annual interest rate, and a number of years. Airgead updates the projection as you edit the fields. Start with figures you can reasonably maintain, then adjust one input to see its effect.

The browser accepts whole-year periods of up to 80 years. It checks input values and displays an error when a calculation cannot be represented safely.

How the calculation works

Airgead divides the annual percentage rate by 100 and then by 12 to obtain a monthly rate. In the deposit scenario, it adds each monthly contribution before calculating that month’s interest. The second scenario applies interest to the starting balance without further deposits.

The process repeats for twelve months per year. Each annual row records the balance, total contributions, and interest earned. Amounts are displayed to two decimal places.

The assumed rate stays constant throughout the calculation. The model does not include taxes, fees, inflation, withdrawals, market losses, or changes in interest rates.

Read your results

The Results page separates the projected ending balance into contributions and interest. It also shows how the same initial amount would grow without monthly deposits.

Use the chart to understand the overall pattern, then read the annual tables for exact reported values. The distinction between contributed money and earned interest is especially important when comparing plans.

Compare investment scenarios

Compare allows two scenarios with independent starting balances, monthly deposits, interest rates, and timeframes. Its chart separates contributed principal from projected interest.

To understand the effect of a particular decision, change one input at a time. For example, compare two monthly deposit amounts while holding the rate and timeframe constant.

Save plans and export results

From Results, you can save a named plan in your current browser, reopen it later, or download the annual figures as CSV. Neither saving nor exporting requires an account.

The website has no account backend. Demo sign-in is not authentication, and saved plans do not sync across devices. Clearing site data may delete your saved plans.
